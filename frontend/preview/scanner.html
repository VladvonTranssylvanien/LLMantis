You've been visited by the LLMantis checker

This page is what our User-Agent string points to. If a request from LLMantis-Checker/1.0 showed up in your server logs, here's exactly what happened and why.

What we did

We sent a single HTTP GET request to a public page on your site — the same request an ordinary visitor's browser makes when they open the page. We looked at the HTML for two things:

  • Is there a chat widget (a known vendor script — Intercom, Tidio, Crisp, and similar)?
  • If so, does the page text disclose that it's an AI, as required by Art. 50(1) of the EU AI Act (in force since 2 August 2026)?

What we did not do

  • We never opened your chat widget or sent it a message.
  • We never attempted to log in, bypass authentication, or access anything not already public.
  • We didn't store your page content — only whether a widget exists and whether it discloses being AI.

Who we are

LLMantis tests whether AI chatbots disclose that they're AI and, for consenting customers, runs documented security attacks against them. This particular check — the free layer — is passive by design: no permission is required because it's equivalent to a normal page visit.

How to opt out

We honour robots.txt. If you'd rather we didn't visit, add a rule disallowing LLMantis-Checker and we won't fetch your pages again. If you have any other concern, write to us — a human reads every message: kontakt@llmantis.de.
