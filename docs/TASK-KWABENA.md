## You have a veto

If any sentence we put in front of a customer overstates what we do, say so and
it does not ship. Use it on marketing copy, on the report template, on the
pitch deck, on the website.

The failure mode here is not a bad user experience. It is an *Abmahnung* — a
formal cease-and-desist with costs — in our first month of trading.

## The line we never cross

We issue a **Prüfbericht** (test report) and a **Nachweis** (evidence of
testing). We are **not** a certification body, and we cannot become one:
conformity certificates come only from notified bodies, and only for high-risk
systems. A chatbot certification does not exist as a legal category.

Banned everywhere — UI text, comments, variable names, documentation, commit
messages: *certified*, *zertifiziert*, *Zertifikat*, *AI-Act-compliant*,
*AI-Act-konform*, *GDPR-compliant*, *DSGVO-konform*, *gesetzlich vorgeschrieben*.

Correct phrasing: **"tested against known LLM vulnerabilities (OWASP LLM Top 10)"**.
